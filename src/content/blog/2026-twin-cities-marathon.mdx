Personally, I love Medal Mondays. Runners around the world know what it means: you
accomplished something amazing over the weekend. For me, it was running the Twin Cities
Marathon Ultra Loony challenge. This challenge meant running both days of the marathon
weekend: the 10k followed by the 5k on Saturday, and the full marathon on Sunday. This was
not an easy task. Still, completing this challenge was a highlight of the weekend; it's
only a small part of what made it special.

<Image
  src={capitol}
  alt="The Minnesota State Capitol lit up in the morning pre-sunrise, with race-weekend tents and signage on the lawn in front"
/>

The first thing that made it so special: a few months ago, my youngest decided they wanted
a challenge. They've run plenty of 5ks, but this time they wanted to run the 10k with me.
Leading up to race day, they were incredibly nervous. I kept reassuring them that I would
never push them to run it if I didn't think they had it in them. In the end, they crushed
their first 10k in 54:30. That is an amazing time, and I can't wait to see where they go
from there. Their older sibling joined us for the 5k that day and set a new PR of 26:25,
so it was a weekend of personal bests all around. Proud of both of them!

<Image
  src={indigefit}
  alt="The Indigefit Kids Marathon Weekend banner over the entrance to a tent area near the State Capitol before sunrise"
/>

In addition, the [Shakopee Mdewakanton Sioux Community (SMSC)](https://www.shakopee.org/)
started a new project called [Indigefit](https://www.indigefit.org/) to empower Indigenous
youth in Minnesota to get out, run, move, and be more active. It was amazing to see all
the Indigenous youth at the Twin Cities 5k on Saturday taking part in the event. Many of
them had never run a 5k before, and the Indigefit instructors helped them reach that goal
over the summer. A huge thank you to the Shakopee Mdewakanton Sioux Community and
[Twin Cities in Motion](https://www.tcmevents.org/) for putting this together. I can't
wait to see where this program goes in the future.

<Image
  src={marathonRun}
  alt="A selfie of me running the marathon in a blue shirt and red-and-white cap, with other runners and a cornfield behind me"
/>

Thank you to Twin Cities in Motion for putting on such a spectacular event. It's one of
America's best urban marathons, and I look forward to running it again.
